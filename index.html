The Simple Bottom Line

For weeks, residents of Honor Tower in Kahlert Village have reported faint tapping, the mysterious smell of microwave popcorn, and what one source described as “a tiny discussion about splitting utilities” coming from the ventilation system. Our completely fictional inquiry has reached one unavoidable conclusion: a community of lizard people has moved into the ducts.

Why the vents? Location. Climate control. Easy access to campus. Most importantly, no application fee, no security deposit, and no requirement to prove an income equal to three times the monthly rent. They did not choose the duct life. The housing market chose it for them.

The Housing Economics

Traditional sun-baked rocks offer warmth but terrible Wi-Fi. Apartments offer plumbing but require money. The Honor Tower vent network, meanwhile, provides warmth, central placement, and a direct acoustic connection to every roommate disagreement in the building.

  • Monthly vent rent: allegedly zero dollars plus lint.
  • Square footage: classified as “cozy” by several imaginary landlords.
  • Amenities: forced air, ambient dining-hall aromas, excellent echoes.
  • Commute: six minutes by tunnel, four minutes if the fan is on.

In other words, the lizard people are not plotting to take over campus. They are waiting for prices to come down. This could take several geological eras.
